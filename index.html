1 Who we are

CoPlanable is operated by CoPlanable Inc., a Wyoming corporation (“CoPlanable,” “we,” “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the CoPlanable app for iPhone and Android (the “App”), how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have.

2 You don’t need an account

You can use CoPlanable without signing up or giving us your name or email address.

3 Information that stays on your device

The plans you create in CoPlanable are saved in the App’s storage on your device only. We don’t upload them to our servers, and we can’t access them. This includes:

  • Your events and plans: event details, templates, budgets, vendors, seating charts, run sheets, tasks and notes.
  • Your guest lists: guest names, party sizes, email addresses, phone numbers, and any dietary or accessibility notes you add.
  • Photos: photos of receipts or vendor documents you take or choose.

The App asks for these permissions only when you use the related feature, and it still works if you say no:

PermissionWhat it’s for
ContactsTo let you pick people from your contacts to add as guests. Only the contacts you choose are copied into your guest list on your device. We never receive your contacts.
CalendarTo add your event dates to your calendar. We never receive your calendar. If you choose a calendar that syncs with an account such as Google or iCloud, the items you add also go to that provider and are covered by its privacy policy, not ours.
Photos and cameraTo add photos of receipts or vendor documents to your plans. The photos stay on your device.
NotificationsTo send you planning reminders. See Section 4.

Please keep in mind:

  • If your device backs up to iCloud, Google or another service, your App data may be included in that backup. Those backups are covered by your backup provider’s privacy policy, not ours.
  • Because your plans live on your device, deleting the App or losing or resetting your device can erase them. We can’t recover them for you.
  • When you add guests, you’re storing information about other people. Please only add details you have a good reason to keep, and only add dietary or accessibility notes with your guest’s agreement.

4 Information we collect

We collect only the following:

What we don’t collect: We don’t collect your name, email (unless you include it in feedback), payment information, your contacts, your calendar, your photos, or anything in your plans or guest lists.

5 How we use information

We use the information in Section 4 to:

  • Run, maintain and fix the App.
  • Understand how people use CoPlanable so we can improve it.
  • Send the push notifications you allow.
  • Read and reply to your feedback and support requests.
  • Keep the App secure and prevent abuse.
  • Meet our legal obligations.

We don’t use your information for advertising, we don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we don’t use it for any other purpose without asking you first.

7 Your choices

  • Permissions: change access to contacts, calendar, photos, camera and notifications anytime in your phone’s settings under CoPlanable.
  • Push notifications: turn them off in your phone’s settings.
  • Feedback: sending feedback is always optional.

8 Keeping and deleting information

  • Your plans: stay on your device until you delete them or delete the App.
  • Feedback and support messages: until deleted
  • Anonymous usage analytics: until deleted
  • Crash reports: until deleted
  • Anonymous device ID: until deleted

9 How we protect information

We encrypt information sent between the App and our service providers, limit access to the small team that needs it, and choose providers with strong security practices. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

10 Age requirement

You must be at least 16 years old to use CoPlanable. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has sent us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11 Where information is processed

CoPlanable Inc. is based in the United States, and our service providers process information in the United States and possibly other countries. If you live in Canada, your information may be stored outside Canada and may be accessible to courts, law enforcement and national security authorities in those countries under their laws.

12 Your privacy rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access the information we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct information that’s wrong.
  • Delete your information.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To make a request, email info@coplanable.com. We’ll respond within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires, and we won’t charge you or treat you differently for using your rights. Because your plans are stored only on your device, you can view, change or delete them in the App at any time, and we have no copy.

If you live in Canada. You have these rights under federal and provincial privacy laws. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or your provincial commissioner, such as Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information.

If you live in the United States. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what we collect and why, to access, correct and delete it, and to opt out of the sale of your information or its use for targeted advertising. We don’t sell your information or use it for targeted advertising. You can use an authorized agent to make a request. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision.

13 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CoPlanable grows, for example if we add accounts or cloud sync. When we do, we’ll change the effective date at the top. If the changes are significant, we’ll tell you in the App before they take effect, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
